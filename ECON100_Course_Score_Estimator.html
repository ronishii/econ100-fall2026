How the estimate is made

About 60% of the course score is already recorded in Canvas. The other 40% comes from the homework, quizzes, workshops, and Exams 3 and 4 that are still ahead.

Estimated course score = 0.6 × your current course score + 0.4 × your average on the remaining work

If you are taking the Exam 2 makeup, your makeup score will replace the 0 now shown for Exam 2 in Canvas. Exam 2 is worth 10% of the course score. So a makeup score of 80 out of 100 adds 8 percentage points to your estimated course score.

The grading scale is A at 90% or above, B at 80%, C at 70%, and D at 60%.
